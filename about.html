Who we are

Fresh food starts with knowing where to find it.

FreshFind brings farmers-market information and seasonal produce discovery together in one accessible browser-based platform.

Farmers markets connect communities with growers and fresh seasonal food, but useful market information can often be scattered across flyers, community boards, social media, and word of mouth.

FreshFind is designed to make that discovery easier. The platform brings market locations, operating days and hours, and the types of produce typically available into one convenient place.

Whether someone wants to find a nearby market or understand what produce is in season, FreshFind keeps the experience simple, searchable, and easy to explore.

Our purpose

To help residents discover local farmers markets and make the most of seasonal local produce through clear, organized, and accessible information.
